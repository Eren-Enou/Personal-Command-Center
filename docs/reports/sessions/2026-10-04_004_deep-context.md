## Decisions and findings

The same abstraction worked for chapter/character, build/location, architecture/backlinks and research/provenance observations. No domain-specific tables or hierarchy were needed. Tags remain global; Topics remain local; formal Notes remain substantial titled writing.

Queries aggregate counts and batch associations: eight SQL statements including BEGIN for both the normal Atlas sample and a 20-note regression test. Parent timestamps remain intact when adding context. Search retains literal substring scans; no new engine or speculative index was added.

Page length is the clearest friction: current fields remain first, but formal documents move below the stream. Provenance Topics do not enforce sources/confidence, and overlapping names such as Research or Frost Mage require users to understand local/global/current-state scope. A compact Context Resume experiment using existing facts and recent observations is the recommended next milestone, subject to daily-use evidence.

## Limitations and outstanding work

Streams/search remain unpaginated; hundreds of observations were not benchmarked. No draft recovery, autosave, concurrency protection, undo, structured evidence, Tag suggestions, nested Topics, attachments or AI infrastructure. Direct SQL can violate service-enforced parent pairs. Topic creation preserves but does not automatically select the new Topic. Activity targets parents and truncates long snapshots.

Milestone 3's requested implementation and validation are complete within the documented native-download observation limit. Changes are local/uncommitted. The normal backend and preview remain running; reports and source are reviewable. Further Context Resume development is a future milestone, not included here.
